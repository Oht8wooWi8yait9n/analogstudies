20-Foot Chamber Analog (Exploration Atmosphere)

Do you have a passion for space? Are you interested in being a human research subject for NASA’s Exploration Atmosphere?

NASA’s Johnson Space Center (JSC) test subject screening is recruiting volunteers for an overnight study lasting up to 15 days where you will live and work with others in an altitude chamber to test procedures that should reduce the risk of decompression sickness in astronauts during spacewalks, also called extravehicular activity (EVA). In brief, selected participants will aid researchers in the validation of a denitrogenation protocol that reduces the risk of decompression sickness during a 6-hour exposure to low atmospheric pressure in an altitude chamber. Volunteers must pass a JSC Class III physical, psychological assessment, physiological training, VO2 Max test, lung capacity test/x-rays, and ultrasound of the heart to qualify and comply with requirements.

Human Research Participant Requirements for Exploration Atmosphere (20-Foot Chamber)

  • Must commit to an up to 15 day mission along with multiple days of pre and post mission activities living at a reduced atmospheric pressure in the 20-foot altitude chamber in Building 7 at NASA’s Johnson Space Center including:
    • 2–3 days of pre-mission screening
    • 2–3 days of pre-mission data collection
    • 5 days pre-mission training, including 1 overnight stay
    • Up to 15 days confined habitation, including 14 overnight stays and 1 pre-chamber training
    • 3 days post-mission testing
    (Total experiment commitment: up to 29 days, including 15 days of confined isolation.)
  • Must be healthy with physical and biological characteristics comparable to those of astronauts currently involved in NASA’s human space flight program.
  • Must be between the ages of 30–60 at mission start time.
  • Must have a bachelor’s degree, and ability to read/speak/comprehend English.
  • Must be physically active and able to achieve a VO2 peak (cycling) of 35 ml/min/kg for Male and 30 ml/min/kg for Female.
  • Must be able to pass all of the screening tests and training activities.

Specific Exclusion Criteria (20-Foot Chamber)

  • Must be a healthy, non-smoking volunteer.
  • Subject does not take over-the-counter or prescription medication to control: a chronic painful condition, anxiety or stress disorder, psychosis, a sleep disorder or a breathing disorder.
  • Must have no dietary restrictions.
  • No respiratory illness.
  • No significant findings on Echocardiogram screening (Atrial Septal Defect, etc.).
  • Must have no history of significant sleep apnea, sleepwalking or taking sleep-aids.
  • Must have US citizenship or be a legal permanent resident (Green Card holder; no foreign nationals).
  • Consent to research studies collecting biological samples and investigating the effects on personal behaviors, team cohesion, cognition, and communication.
  • Pregnancy is an exclusion criterion.

**Volunteers are compensated. Some restrictions apply to NASA civil servants and contractors. Contact your Human Resources department for your company's policy.
